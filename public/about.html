ABOUT THE DAILY BRIEF

changing world.

We cover the ideas, decisions and businesses shaping the global economy—with curiosity, context and care.

The Daily Brief is an editorial newsroom focused on the intersections of business, policy, technology and capital. Our journalists look beyond the headline to explain what is changing, why it matters and what to watch next.

Our coverage is produced by an editorial team and is separate from financial products and investment advice.
